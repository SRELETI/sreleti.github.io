I'm Sudeep Reddy Eleti, a Lead Infrastructure Engineer at [BenchSci](https://www.benchsci.com/) in Toronto. I joined in 2022 and moved into the lead role in January 2026.

## What I work on

Most of my time goes into Emet, BenchSci's agentic system for scientists. Think of it as Cursor for the lab. My parts of it:

* **Agent development:** building the system and running it in production.
* **Evaluation:** red-teaming.
* **Operations:** observability and incident management for the live system.

## Outside work

I mostly build low-level and infrastructure things: a filesystem written from scratch on a raw block device, a code-execution sandbox for AI agents, and a daily booking-slot limit for the open-source cal.diy scheduler.
